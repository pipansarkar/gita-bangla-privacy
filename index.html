Gita Bangla Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Gita Bangla is published by focustech. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information. The app has no account, advertising or analytics, and it does not have permission to access the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your bookmarks, notes, reading progress, reading plan, display settings and reminder settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. You can erase them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Android backup

If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this app's stored settings and notes in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by your device settings and Google's terms. We cannot access that backup.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules them on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Sharing

When you choose to share a verse, Android's share screen sends the image and text to the app you select. That app's privacy policy then applies.

Text-to-speech

The read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech service installed on your device. Only verse text is sent to that service.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/pipansarkar/gita-bangla-privacy/issues.
